Everything you need to know about selling your Kansas City home for cash. Don't see your question? Call or text us at (816) 973-5420.

How do I sell my house fast in Kansas City?

The fastest way to sell a house in Kansas City is to work directly with a local cash home buyer like Heartland Acquisitions. Submit your address, schedule a quick walkthrough, and you'll have a no-obligation cash offer within 1 hour. From there, you can close in as little as 7 days at a local title company. No listings, no showings, no waiting on financing.

How do you decide what to offer for my house?

We look at comparable properties that have recently sold in your neighborhood, factor in your home's condition and any updates, then account for our holding and renovation costs plus a fair margin. Our goal is a number that nets you more than what would be left after agent fees, repairs, and concessions on a traditional sale.

Are there really no fees or commissions?

Correct. No commissions, no listing fees, no closing costs charged to you. We cover the title and closing fees on our side. The number on the offer is the number that hits your bank account at closing.

What types of houses do you buy in Kansas City?

We buy single-family homes, duplexes, and small multi-family properties across the entire Kansas City metro. That includes Jackson, Clay, Cass, and Platte counties on the Missouri side, plus Wyandotte and Johnson counties on the Kansas side. Vacant, occupied, tenanted, condemned, fire-damaged, hoarder houses, we've seen it all and we'll still make a fair offer.

Can you buy my house if it needs major repairs?

Absolutely. Distressed houses are our specialty. Roof damage, foundation issues, outdated plumbing or electrical, hoarder situations, fire or water damage, even properties with code violations. We buy houses as-is and handle every repair on our end. You don't fix, paint, clean, or even haul anything out. Leave whatever you don't want.

How fast can we close on my house?

As quickly as a local title company can clear title, usually 7 to 14 days from accepted offer. If you need more time to find a new place or sort out belongings, we'll set whatever closing date works for you. Up to 60 days isn't a problem. You set the timeline, not us.

Do I have to clean out my house before selling?

No. Take what you want and leave the rest. Furniture, appliances, boxes in the basement, the riding mower in the garage. We'll handle disposal as part of our renovation. This is one of the biggest reliefs for sellers dealing with inherited property or downsizing situations.

Can I sell a house with tenants in it?

Yes. We regularly buy rental properties with tenants in place. We can honor existing leases or work with you on a transition plan, and we never ask the seller to handle eviction or move-out. Tired landlords, this is for you.

How do I sell an inherited house in Kansas City?

Once probate clears (or if the property was held in a trust), you can sell to a cash buyer like us with minimal hassle. We're familiar with the paperwork on inherited properties, can coordinate directly with your attorney, and can handle out-of-state sellers entirely remotely. Most inherited-house sellers find this far easier than listing.

Are you a wholesaler or the actual buyer?

We are the buyer. Heartland Acquisitions purchases properties with our own funds, then renovates and either rents or resells them. No assigning contracts to a third party, no surprise faces at closing, no daisy chain of investors. The name on the contract is the name that closes.

How is selling to Heartland different from a traditional realtor?

A realtor lists your house publicly, shows it to dozens of strangers over weeks or months, and depends on a buyer who needs financing approval. You pay 6% in commissions plus closing costs and concessions. Selling to us is private, fast, and certain. One walkthrough, a cash offer within an hour, and a closing date you choose. No fees, no repairs, no contingencies.

What areas of Kansas City do you serve?

The entire Kansas City metro on both sides of the state line. That includes Kansas City MO, Independence, Lee's Summit, Blue Springs, Raytown, Grandview, Belton, Liberty, Gladstone, Kansas City KS, Overland Park, Olathe, Shawnee, Lenexa, and surrounding suburbs.
